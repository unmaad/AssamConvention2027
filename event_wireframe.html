About the event

A Celebration Three Years in the Making

Bringing together communities from across the world, this festival celebrates the richness of our shared heritage through music, dance, cuisine, fashion, and storytelling. Every edition deepens connections and creates memories that last a lifetime.

Open to families, cultural enthusiasts, and international visitors alike, the event is recognized by participating nations and supported by international cultural bodies as a landmark in the global cultural calendar.
